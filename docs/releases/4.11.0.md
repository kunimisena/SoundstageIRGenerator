# Soundstage IR Generator 4.11.0

## 中文

这版整理了本轮试听确认的 EQ、混响占比和 FABIAN 修正。

- **EQ 与带通一起求解。** 从原始四路径响应出发，平滑求逆并纳入 20 Hz–20 kHz 带通目标，修正低频过渡带异常抬升的问题。校正与输出长度使用明确的有限预算，避免校正尾部反复延长；20 Hz 和 20 kHz 属于通带。
- **保住设置的混响占比。** 预估 EQ 对直达声与混响能量的不同影响，求解一个共同的混响增益，再合成最终核。所有反射源和频段共用这个标量，保留混响原有的相对能量分布。结果中显示最终实测占比。
- **FABIAN 使用实测单耳镜像。** 以左耳全方向数据生成两耳响应，替代此前左右镜像响应的复数平均，消除平均操作引入的额外相消。原始耳廓响应、相对时差及共同频响补偿仍保留。
- **混响尾端平滑收尾。** 各频带在 −70 至 −80 dB 区间渐隐，并重新标定目标能量。
- **分析图可切换带通范围。** 默认查看可听频段；勾选“显示带通范围”后查看带外及更低的幅度范围。
- **一级 EQ 默认平滑改为 1/12 oct。** 二级仍为 1/3 oct；载入配置保留其中记录的设置。

升级后重新生成卷积核，才会应用这些改动。相同配置在新版中的响应可能不同，尤其是 FABIAN 镜像及 EQ 后混响占比。共同输出归一化保持既定逻辑。

下载 `SoundstageIRGenerator-4.11.0-win-x64.zip`，解压后运行文件夹内的程序。源码包和 SHA-256 校验文件也随版本提供。歌曲处理可选择本机 FFmpeg 路径。FABIAN 及运行时许可说明随包保留。

## English

This release brings together the auditioned EQ, wet-balance and FABIAN corrections.

- **Joint EQ and bandpass synthesis:** smoothed inversion of the original four paths includes the 20 Hz–20 kHz target, correcting excessive gain in the infrasonic transition. A finite support budget replaces repeated EQ-tail extension. Both 20 Hz and 20 kHz are in the passband.
- **Post-EQ wet-balance compensation:** the solver predicts the different energy changes in direct and reverberant components and adjusts one common wet gain before final synthesis. Relative spectral and directional energy distributions are preserved. Results report the measured final wet percentage.
- **Single-ear FABIAN mirroring:** the measured left-ear full-sphere data generates both ears, replacing complex averaging of mirrored measurements and removing cancellation introduced by that average. Original pinna responses, relative timing and common-transfer compensation are retained.
- **Smooth tail termination:** each reverberation band fades between −70 and −80 dB and is recalibrated to its target energy.
- **Optional bandpass plot range:** the default view focuses on audible frequencies; the checkbox reveals the transition and stop bands.
- **First-stage smoothing defaults to 1/12 octave:** the second stage remains at 1/3 octave. Loaded configurations retain their stored values.

Regenerate kernels to apply the changes. The same configuration can produce different responses, particularly with FABIAN and post-EQ wet balancing. Common output normalization retains the established behavior.

Download and extract `SoundstageIRGenerator-4.11.0-win-x64.zip`, then run the executable inside. Source and SHA-256 packages are also provided. Song processing accepts a local FFmpeg path. FABIAN and runtime license notices are included.
