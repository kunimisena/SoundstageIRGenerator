Modifications by Soundstage IR Generator:
- float32 embedded table; original SOFA sample timing and gain retained.
- nearest directional sample on the original approximately 2-degree grid.
- measured left-ear HRIRs over the full sphere form the anatomical reference;
  the right ear uses the left-ear HRIR at the mirrored source direction.
  Selected IRs retain their complex response and time reference without averaging.
  The same symmetric head is used in both random-mirror modes.
- optional official 1/3-octave smoothed minimum-phase inverse CTF convolution.
- 44.1/48/96 kHz windowed-sinc sample-rate conversion with explicit common lead-in.
- no individual path normalization, independent peak alignment, or minimum-phase
  reconstruction of the measured HRIR; room synthesis and final EQ are separate.
Dataset limitations: blocked-ear-canal entrance measurements. Frequencies below
200 Hz and elevations below -64 degrees include numerical substitutes; the
200-500 Hz transition combines measured and numerical magnitude and phase.
This is not an individual listener HRTF or a biological bone-conduction model.
Source and converted table SHA256 values are in FABIAN-manifest.json.
